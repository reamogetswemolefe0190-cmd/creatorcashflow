# Creator Cash Flow

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary buyer and daily operator is a smaller South African creator or marketing agency managing brand campaigns. Brand reviewers and assigned creators participate in the same campaign with narrower, role-specific views. Creators also have a separate private financial-record tool.

## Product Purpose

Make a campaign's brief, creator assignment, deliverables, review decisions, published links and results visible in one workflow. The primary operating question is who needs to do what next. Agencies should be able to run a real campaign with less status chasing across spreadsheets, email and messages.

## Positioning

Agency workflow first: one shared campaign record with distinct agency, brand and creator responsibilities. CCF is not a creator-discovery network or an automated payments platform. The first market fit is a hypothesis to validate through agency pilots, not an established claim.

## Operating Context

An agency creates the brief, assigns a registered creator and requests brand review access. The creator accepts the assignment, submits draft and published links, and may enter labelled results. Agency or brand reviewers approve or request revisions. Campaign participants can see their permitted information; unrelated creator finances remain private.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The local, unshipped workflow slice has campaign creation, role-scoped access, assignment consent, brand-access acceptance, deliverable transitions and manually entered results. It needs database migrations and production pilot validation before being called live.
- The interactive public demo uses sample data and does not notify participants, move money or verify performance.
- No automatic invitation email, file upload, platform-verified campaign metrics, payment transfer, automatic invoice execution or production reporting is currently promised.
- The previous PDF report generator is shelved. A report should be redesigned only after pilot users validate the information they need.
- The creator ledger is a separate private tool, not the agency's campaign-finance view.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the name and logo, a light visual feel, premium restraint, legible color relationships and dedicated mobile composition. The user has identified Stitch as a reference for the way a light palette feels cohesive, not as permission to copy its assets.

## Evidence on Hand

- `docs/CAMPAIGN_WORKFLOW_IMPLEMENTATION.md` documents what the unshipped workflow does and does not do.
- `docs/CREATORIQ_CCF_AUDIT_2026-09-23.md` records the market hypothesis, limits and suggested pilot test.
- The public sample campaign and role previews exist in the app. There is no confirmed customer case study or validated ROI claim.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the next accountable action, not decorative analytics.
2. Show the same campaign through clear role-specific permissions.
3. Label sample, manual and platform-sourced information distinctly.
4. Keep creator finances private and separate from campaign operations.
5. Preserve a useful manual workflow even without Meta access or payment integrations.
