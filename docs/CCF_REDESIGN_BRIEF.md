# CCF campaign desk redesign

Scope: public introduction, agency workspace, brand workspace, creator assignment view and shared campaign. Persuade on the public page; Operate inside the product.

Audience: small South African creator agencies first. Brand reviewers and creators enter through a campaign they have permission to see. Their task is to understand the current handoff and complete one real action.

Confirmed product boundary: the workflow is locally implemented but unshipped, and results entered by participants are manual. The public preview is sample data. Creator financial records stay private. No automatic payment, platform-verified campaign report or email invitation is implied.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** CCF is a production desk for a campaign, not a dashboard of hypothetical KPIs. Replace metric-card-first pages with a ranked action queue and an ordered campaign file.

**OWN-WORLD:** A bright studio work surface: white and quiet warm paper, near-black type, a single orange action signal, precise rules, restrained 12–16px surfaces, and one consistent control language. Information sits on the page rather than inside nested cards.

**STORY:** An agency sees what is waiting, opens a campaign, assigns the next owner, and records the decision. A brand sees only review requests and permitted campaign evidence. A creator sees assignments and deliverables, never the agency's aggregate fee or another creator's work.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Agency: a direct “Work needing a decision” title, primary campaign action, then a real campaign queue. Brand: pending access or review first. Creator: assignments first; private money tools remain a separate navigation destination. Campaign: name and current handoff above deliverables, with setup and history secondary.

**FORM:** Campaign dossier / production desk, the third grounded direction in concept seed `5ca06624`. The signature interaction is a current-handoff rail that advances with saved decisions. Public scroll reveals explain the same sequence without hiding content by default. Product interactions are immediate, with motion only for changed state.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Implementation assumption: proceed code-first using the campaign-dossier concept frame as a critique reference. The user confirmed agency workflow first but did not choose a separate build-path preference or an alternate structural direction. Those remain easy to revisit without changing product truth.
